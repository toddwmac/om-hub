Despite the AI boom, core failure reasons have remained remarkably consistent over the past decade. Lack of product-market fit continues to be the leading cause of death for startups.

AI-era nuance:

Many AI startups are failing due to over-reliance on hype rather than solving real customer problems. “AI-washing” and commoditized tools have led to rapid churn and weak retention.
